Keeping this on my phone, offline

iPhone: open this page in Safari, tap the share button, then Add to Home Screen. Open it once from the icon while you still have a signal. After that it opens without data.

Android: open in Chrome, tap the three dots, then Add to Home screen, and open it once from the icon.

If the signal fails anyway: keep the downloaded prayerbook.html file in your Files or Downloads folder. Tapping it opens this same prayerbook with no internet at all.

Marked prayers, pauses, text size and night reading are remembered on this phone only.
